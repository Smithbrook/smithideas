Policies

Last updated: July 2026

Smith Ideas ("Smith Ideas," "we," "us") builds a small set of independent apps and tools. This page covers privacy and basic terms for all of them. If an app links here, this policy applies to it.

Privacy

What we collect

Depending on the app, we may collect:

  • Email address — if you sign up, subscribe, or contact support.
  • Payment information — handled entirely by Stripe and/or Gumroad. We never see or store your full card details.
  • Basic usage data — things like page views, feature usage, or error logs, used to keep apps running, improve them, and fix bugs.

Some apps are built to work without an account and may store data only in your browser or device. Where that's the case, we don't have access to that data.

How we use it

We use the information above to:

  • Provide, maintain, and improve the app you're using
  • Process payments and manage subscriptions or licenses
  • Respond to support requests
  • Send emails related to your use of our apps, such as receipts, service updates, and occasional, relevant news

We don't sell, rent, or trade your personal information with third parties. We only share it with the services we rely on to run the apps. You can unsubscribe from non-essential emails or request removal of your information at any time by following the instructions in our emails or contacting us at support@smithideas.com.

Payments

Payments are processed by Stripe and/or Gumroad, depending on the app. They handle your payment details directly under their own privacy policies. Smith Ideas never stores full card numbers.

Third-party services

We rely on a small number of trusted services (for example: payment processors, hosting providers, and error reporting tools) to run these apps. Each only receives the data it needs to do its job, and each processes that data under its own policies.

Data retention

We keep information for as long as your account, subscription, or license is active, or as needed to meet legal, tax, and accounting requirements. You can request deletion of your account or personal data at any time (see Contact below).

Children's privacy

Our apps are not directed at children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect data from them. If you believe a child under 13 has provided us with personal information, contact us and we'll delete it.

We may update this policy as Smith Ideas grows. Material changes will be reflected here with an updated date.
